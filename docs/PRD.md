# Pulse product requirements

The supplied master specification governs the PostgreSQL edition. The academy needs to follow a student observation from collection to a reviewed theme, accountable owner and later measurement.

Students receive targeted survey invitations, answer rating, text, single-choice and multiple-choice questions, and see submission confirmation. The academy chooses named or anonymous collection per survey. Staff schedule publication, configure sample and reminder policies, monitor response rates, and archive surveys. Lecturers see their own surveys and assigned improvements. Leads act on feedback in their domain. Directors see academy-wide results. Administrators manage the directory and system settings.

A background worker delivers invitations, reminders and analysis jobs. The AI provider returns structured themes, sentiment, recommendations and summaries backed by actual responses. Human review precedes publication. An authorized person explicitly creates an issue, selects an owner and deadline, and tracks its history and resolution. Subsequent compatible surveys provide evidence of improvement.

Reports show eligible ratings, coverage, theme counts, deadlines and historical changes. Comparisons must use the same scale and canonical criterion and meet the minimum sample. The product supports CSV and printable PDF reports, role-scoped APIs, anonymous identity separation, tests, local development mail, and deployment through Docker Compose.

Acceptance requires a working PostgreSQL-backed frontend-to-backend journey, tested permissions and duplicate prevention, working jobs, migrations, production builds and documented operational limits.
